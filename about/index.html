Why We Created 844.THY.SONG™

Helping people hear Scripture in a new way.

We believe Scripture can reach people in powerful ways...especially when the words they already love become something they can listen to again and again.

What if the Scripture passages that mean the most to us could become songs we actually wanted to listen to again and again?

Scripture first.

844.THY.SONG™ starts with the Bible passage you choose. You choose the sound too. Then we create a custom song from those words.

Simple on purpose.

No app. No account. No music software. You text the Scripture passage and the style you want, and we text the finished song back to you.

Made to feel personal.

A familiar passage can feel different when you hear it through music. Our goal is to help people experience Scripture personally, remember it, return to it, and hear words they know in a fresh way.

Made for sharing.

Scripture songs can be shared with family, friends, children, grandparents, church members, or someone who simply needs encouragement. They can also become meaningful gifts because the recipient gets to choose the Scripture and music that matter to them.
