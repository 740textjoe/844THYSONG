Gift Questions

Am I choosing the Scripture passage for them?

No. The recipient chooses the Scripture passages and music styles they want after redeeming the gift code.

Are these pre-made songs?

No. Each song is created from the recipient's chosen Scripture passage and music style.

How do I give them the code?

With a digital gift, you receive the code immediately and can share it however you choose. When physical gift ordering opens, we will include the code in the package mailed directly to the recipient.

Can you mail the gift directly to them?

Physical gift mailing is coming soon. Once it opens, you will be able to enter the recipient's mailing address and we will mail the 844.THY.SONG™ coaster, your personal message, and their unique gift code directly to them.

Do I get the gift code too?

Yes. Digital gift buyers receive the code after purchase. When physical gift ordering opens, the buyer will also receive the code as a backup while we mail the gift directly to the recipient.

Does the recipient need an app or account?

No. THYSONG works by text message. No app, account, or music software is required.

Is this a good Christian gift for someone who has everything?

Yes. Instead of giving another object, you are giving them the chance to create custom songs from Scripture passages that matter to them. It can be especially meaningful for someone who is difficult to shop for.

Is this appropriate for First Communion, Confirmation, Baptism, or graduation?

Yes. A THYSONG gift can mark major Christian milestones and life transitions because the recipient chooses the Scripture and music style that are meaningful to them.
